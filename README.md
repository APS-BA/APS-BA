## Projects

### 🏨 [Hotel Cancellation Intelligence](https://github.com/APS-BA/hotel-cancellation-intelligence)
A cancellation-risk model turned into a running system: scores new bookings weekly,
monitors its own accuracy and drift, retrains itself, and passes each week to an
LLM analyst whose every number is checked against the model output.
`Python` `scikit-learn` `Streamlit` `Claude / Gemini API` `prompt design`

### 💳 [Personal-Loan Offer Amount Model · TVS Credit EPIC 8](https://github.com/APS-BA/tvs-credit-loan-offer-model)
Re-sized pre-approved loan offers on what each customer has proved they can repay,
modulated by a LightGBM risk score chosen on holdout, not cross-validation. Scores
89.6/100 on the brief's metric against 60.0 for the incumbent logic, on the same book.
`Python` `LightGBM` `credit risk` `policy design`

### 🛒 [E-Commerce Returns Analysis](https://github.com/APS-BA/ecommerce-returns-analysis)
Team project on a 6-table e-commerce dataset. Predictive models, statistical tests,
and a cost-based decision model for reducing returns, including an honest
near-random model result that we tested against chance instead of over-claiming.
`Python` `scikit-learn` `statistics` `decision modelling`

### 🏬 [Dealership Sales Analytics](https://github.com/APS-BA/dealership-sales-analytics)
Three years of real invoice data from a consumer-durables dealership. Two
reconciliation catches turned a "collapse and recovery" story into steady erosion,
and showed the only growing category is the least cross-sold.
`Python` `market basket` `RFM` `reproducible analysis`

### 🏢 [Corporate Real Estate Strategy · HCLTech Internship](https://github.com/APS-BA/hcltech-cre-strategy-case-study)

demand for 9 cities × 9 business lines to 2029; built a consolidation cost model.
Case study only; company data stays private.
`forecasting` `benchmarking` `Excel modelling`

### 🚗 [Driver-Based FY2026 Budget · Maruti Suzuki](https://github.com/APS-BA/maruti-suzuki-fy26-budget-model)

### 📊 [Outlook Analytics Modules](https://github.com/APS-BA/outlook-analytics-modules)
